About me

Treating the injury and giving each patient their movement back

I am an orthopedic surgeon, trained at Hadassah Hebrew University Hospital in Jerusalem, Israel, and a medical graduate of Universidad Católica de Santiago de Guayaquil.

Before my specialty training, I completed my internship at Jackson Memorial Hospital through the William J. Harrington Latin American Training Program. I then served as research director in Dr. Carlos Lavernia's Division of Arthritis Surgery, where I worked on scientific articles and papers for several journals and for presentations at medical meetings.

I have practiced surgery in Guayaquil and Samborondón since 2006. Since August 2021, I have been Head of the Orthopedic Surgery Department at UEESCLINIC.

My practice focuses on knee surgery, especially the arthroscopic treatment of anterior cruciate ligament, meniscus and cartilage injuries. I also treat shoulder conditions, fractures and sports injuries.

I remain committed to medical education as Adjunct Faculty of the William J. Harrington Program at the University of Miami.

Frequently asked questions

Before your appointment

When should I see an orthopedic surgeon?
  • If you suffer an injury, a fall or an accident, seek care immediately.
  • If pain in a joint, muscle or bone starts suddenly with no obvious injury and lasts more than 3 days.
  • If there is swelling, if you cannot put weight on your foot or move your arm, or if you felt a pop when you got hurt.

An early diagnosis keeps the injury from getting worse.

What should I bring to my appointment?

Your ID or passport, any tests you have (X-rays, MRIs, ultrasounds and their reports) and a list of the medicines you take. If you have insurance, bring your policy details too.

What is arthroscopy?

It is surgery done through two or three small incisions, using a camera to see inside the joint and repair the meniscus, ligaments or cartilage. Patients usually go home the same day or the next.
