Orthopedic Surgeon

What an orthopedic surgeon does

An orthopedic surgeon treats injuries and conditions of the bones, joints, ligaments, tendons and muscles. That covers everything from a sprain on the field to a fracture that needs surgery, as well as chronic knee, shoulder or back pain.

In Samborondón, Dr. Alberto Sadun Rodríguez offers a full clinical assessment and a treatment plan explained without rushing. Each case starts by understanding how the injury happened, what it keeps the patient from doing day to day and what tests they already have. The goal is to treat the cause of the problem and help recover movement, not just ease the symptom for a while.

When to see a doctor

It is a good idea to see an orthopedic surgeon when:

  • You had an injury, a fall or an accident: in that case, seek care immediately.
  • Pain in a joint, muscle or bone starts suddenly, with no obvious injury, and lasts more than three days.
  • There is swelling, you cannot put weight on your foot or move your arm, or you felt a pop when you got hurt.
  • You have knee, shoulder or back pain that gets in the way of work, sports or sleep.
  • You have sprains, tendinitis, muscle tears or overuse injuries that are not getting better.
  • You were diagnosed with osteoarthritis or osteoporosis and need guidance to prevent complications or fractures.

An early diagnosis keeps the injury from getting worse. If you are not sure what kind of appointment you need, message us on WhatsApp and we will point you in the right direction.

What is treated

Conditions treated in Dr. Sadun's practice include, among others:

  • Knee and arthroscopy: anterior cruciate ligament reconstruction, meniscus repair and trimming, cartilage injuries and assessment of knee osteoarthritis.
  • Shoulder: fractures, dislocations and shoulder pain.
  • Fractures and trauma: cast or splint treatment, and internal fixation surgery when needed.
  • Sports and muscle injuries: sprains, tendinitis, muscle tears and overuse injuries.
  • Back pain: assessment of neck, mid-back and lower back pain, with treatment aimed at the cause.
  • Osteoarthritis and osteoporosis: medical management and fracture prevention.

Care is provided at UEESCLINIC Teaching Hospital, on the campus of Universidad de Especialidades Espíritu Santo in Samborondón, close to patients from Samborondón and Guayaquil.

What care and recovery are like, in general terms

The first appointment includes a review of your history, a physical exam and, when needed, ordering or reviewing X-rays, MRIs or other tests. From there a plan is set: home care, medication, physical therapy, immobilization or, when indicated, surgery.

If treatment is surgical, the UEESCLINIC team works with specialized instruments and patient safety protocols. Recovery varies with the injury: a fracture treated with a cast is handled differently from a knee arthroscopy or shoulder surgery. In every case you are told how to care for yourself, which warning signs to watch for and when your follow-ups are.

Timelines and results are not promised as the same for everyone. The plan is adjusted to your age, activity, health and type of injury.

Why choose Dr. Alberto Sadun

Dr. Alberto Sadun Rodríguez has been Head of the Orthopedic Surgery Department at UEESCLINIC Teaching Hospital since August 2021. He holds a Doctor of Medicine and Surgery degree from Universidad Católica de Santiago de Guayaquil (1999) and became a specialist in orthopedic surgery in 2006, after his residency at Hadassah Hebrew University Hospital in Jerusalem, Israel.

Before his specialty training he completed his internship at Jackson Memorial Hospital through the University of Miami's William J. Harrington Program for Latin America. He also served as research director in Dr. Carlos Lavernia's Division of Arthritis Surgery. Since 2007 he has been Adjunct Faculty of that same Harrington Program.

He has practiced surgery in Guayaquil and Samborondón since 2006. From 2006 to 2021 he worked at Hospital Clínica Kennedy Alborada and, from 2011 to 2014, also at Medikal. His current practice focuses on knee surgery and arthroscopy, and also covers the shoulder, fractures and sports injuries.

Frequently asked questions

Before your appointment

When should I see an orthopedic surgeon?

If you had an accident or a fall, seek care immediately. Also if joint, muscle or bone pain lasts more than three days, or if there is swelling, you cannot put weight on the leg, or you felt a pop when you got hurt.

Does Dr. Sadun only operate on knees?

No. His special focus is knee arthroscopy, but he also treats the shoulder, fractures, sports injuries, back pain, osteoarthritis and osteoporosis.
