Privacy

Appointment form

On the home page, the form “Request an appointment” asks for your name, the reason for the visit and a message you write yourself. When you submit it, the browser opens WhatsApp with a message already written that includes those details. You choose whether to send it.

This website does not store that message. It also does not store the name, the reason or the text. There is no database and no server of this site that receives the form.

WhatsApp

WhatsApp is a separate service from this website. It has its own terms and its own privacy policy. Once the message is sent, the conversation stays in WhatsApp and not on drsadun.org.
