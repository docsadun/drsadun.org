Privacy

Appointment form

On the home page, the form “Request an appointment” asks for your name, the reason for the visit and a message you write yourself. When you submit it, the browser opens WhatsApp with a message already written that includes those details. You choose whether to send it.

This website does not store that message. It also does not store the name, the reason or the text. There is no database and no server of this site that receives the form.

WhatsApp

WhatsApp is a separate service from this website. It has its own terms and its own privacy policy. Once the message is sent, the conversation stays in WhatsApp and not on drsadun.org.

WhatsApp terms of service

WhatsApp privacy policy

Google map

The home page shows a Google Maps map to help you find the office. The map is served by Google directly: when your browser loads it, Google may receive technical data, such as your IP address and browser type, and use cookies under its own privacy policy. This website does not receive that data.

If you prefer not to load the map, the office address is written in the contact section. Google privacy policy

What this website stores in your browser

This website does not use its own cookies or any analytics or advertising tools. It only stores the language you chose, Spanish or English, in your browser so it can show you the same version next time.
